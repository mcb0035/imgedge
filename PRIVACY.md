# ImgEdge Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-01_

ImgEdge is a browser extension plus a local classifier that hides images of a
chosen category (by default, arachnids). It is designed to run **entirely on
your own computer**. This policy explains exactly what it does and does not do
with your data.

**Short version:** ImgEdge has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no
tracking. Your browsing and image content are analyzed locally and are never
sent to the developer or any third party. The only network requests ImgEdge
itself makes are (1) a one-time download of the open-source model files from
GitHub during setup, and (2) fetching an image from the **same website you are
already viewing** so it can be classified locally.

## Who is responsible

ImgEdge is a free, open-source project maintained by Matthew Bedford. Questions:
open an issue at <https://github.com/mcb0035/imgedge>.

## What ImgEdge processes — and where

- **Images on pages you visit.** To decide whether to hide an image, the local
  classifier (running at `127.0.0.1:8723` on your machine) decodes the image and
  runs a model on it. This happens **on your device**; only a block/allow
  verdict is produced.
- **Image URLs and on-screen sizes.** The extension passes an image's URL and
  rendered size to the **local** classifier so it can fetch and score the image.
- **Nothing is sent to us.** ImgEdge has no backend. The developer never
  receives your URLs, your images, or any usage data.

## Network activity (full disclosure)

ImgEdge makes only these outbound requests, and none of them go to the
developer:

1. **Model download (one-time setup).** `imgedge-download-models` downloads the
   open-source iNaturalist (and optional timm) model files from GitHub, each
   verified against a pinned SHA-256 hash.
2. **Image fetch for classification.** When classifying, the local server may
   fetch the image from **the website that hosts it** — the same host your
   browser already contacted to display the page. It uses a generic
   browser-like `User-Agent`, follows no redirects, and contacts only public
   hosts. You can avoid this second request by enabling **“Send image bytes,”**
   which hands the already-loaded image to the classifier instead.

No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising, or third-party SDKs are
included.

## What is stored locally (and how to clear it)

All of this stays on your computer:

- **Settings & lists** (enabled state, detection mode, endpoint, tuning sliders,
  and your allow/block lists) — in the browser's local extension storage
  (`chrome.storage.local`), on this device only; they are never synced to an
  account or sent anywhere.
- **Access token** — generated by the local server and stored in
  `~/.imgedge_token` (owner-only) and in extension storage; it only authorizes
  the local classifier.
- **Verdict cache** — `~/.imgedge_cache.json`, keyed by a keyed hash (HMAC) of
  the URL and storing only the verdict (not the URL or the image).
- **Log file** — a local, size-capped, rotating log that never contains the
  token.

To remove everything: clear your lists in the popup, uninstall the extension,
and delete `~/.imgedge_token`, `~/.imgedge_cache.json`, and `~/.imgedge.log`.

**Exporting/importing your settings.** The popup's **Advanced → Backup** lets you
save your setup to a JSON file and load it back. The exported file contains
**only** your detection mode, tuning sliders, toggles, and endpoint — it
deliberately **excludes your access token and your allow/block lists** (which may
contain URLs), so it holds no secrets and no browsing data and is safe to share
or move between browsers. Importing a file applies only those same fields, keeps
your existing token, and ignores any endpoint that isn't on `localhost`, so an
imported file cannot leak a secret or point the classifier off your machine.

## Permissions and why they are needed

- **Access to the websites you visit** (`host` permissions / `<all_urls>`) —
  required to find images on the page and replace blocked ones. ImgEdge does not
  read page text or form input.
- **`storage`** — to save your settings, lists, and the classifier token.
- **`contextMenus`** — to add the right-click “Allow/Block this image” actions.

## Data sharing and selling

None. ImgEdge does not share, sell, or transfer any data to anyone. No third
parties are involved in its operation.

## Children

ImgEdge collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your choices

You can disable filtering, edit the allow/block lists, choose whether image
bytes are sent to the local classifier, change which category is filtered,
export or import your (token-free) settings, or uninstall at any time.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change and the new
version will be committed to the repository; material changes will be noted in
the project changelog.
